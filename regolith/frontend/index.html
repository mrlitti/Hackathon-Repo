    How it works

    1. Take a reading. Set the sliders to your APXS values, or press Simulate to load a real rover measurement with its instrument noise.
    2. Find match. Regolith compares it with all 2,475 APXS measurements and tells you whether to sample, what the rock likely is, and why.
    3. Look ahead. See the likely minerals, what is within 1 km, and the water in the soil. On the map, turn on an element map or prospect zones.

    Built only from NASA and Canadian Space Agency data. See the Data & methods tab for every source and the accuracy tests.
